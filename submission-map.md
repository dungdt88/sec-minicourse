# Where each submission answer comes from

Content version 2026-10-25.13. The same map is in the content file as the top-level `submission` list. Template: "(Bản nộp) Business Idea & Plan Template 2026" (Milestones 3 and 4, due November 3).

The children answer in Vietnamese in the game. Trí and the mentor then turn those answers into the English template. Question ids (L1F1 and so on) match the game's console and its CSV/JSON exports. H2 is the one optional homework task, "Chơi thử với bạn" (3 friends play the paper draft at school). "Zoom" is the team call after the seven levels.

Every one of the 27 form questions feeds at least one template section. The quizzes are for learning only and feed nothing.

## What changed in content .9 to close gaps

- **L3F3:** now a list of at least 3 alternatives, each with what is good and what is not, plus where the team's game is better. Before, it took three bare names, which could not fill the competitor table.
- **L5F3:** adds an optional amount, so indirect costs have a number.
- **L6F4:** adds "Vì sao đội chọn việc tốt đó?". The rubric rewards explaining why.
- **L7F2:** adds "Việc đó bao lâu làm một lần?" for the "how often" column.

## Things the course does not collect

These are fine for adults to supply:
- Team and school facts.
- The SDG tick.
- The timescale and signatures on the profit agreement.
- USD conversion.
- Splitting costs into direct and indirect.
- Real prices, and how long tasks take.

## Since content .12: less homework

The homework is now one optional task (H2), because the children already have school homework. The recess tally (H3), the price check (H4), the paper-draft log (H1) and "what to change" as a separate task (H5) are gone. "What to change" is now a box inside H2, and parents check prices. The cost: the customer research and problem sections lean on the children's own views unless the playtest happens, so encourage it if the team makes a paper draft.
