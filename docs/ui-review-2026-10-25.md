# UI review of the child screens, 25 October

Every child screen was captured on a phone (iPhone 13 and SE sizes) and a laptop, in both
skins, before and after. The aim: calmer screens, clearer next steps, more picture book.

## What changed

| Screen | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top bar (level, homework) | Small map icon in an outlined circle; the title wrapped to two lines on the worksheet ("Màn 1 · Từ điển pháp / thuật"); "✓ Đã lưu" always shown as a pill | Big map button; a "Màn 1" pill plus where the child is, on one line; saved is a quiet green tick (saving and offline still say so in words) |
| Progress | Dots | A short bar with the ghost riding its end (DR-08), or a knob in the plain skin; "Thẻ 2/4" stays as text |
| "Xem lại thẻ" | Took a whole row on every worksheet screen | Sits beside the progress bar (and beside the review title) |
| Back / Next | Pushed below the fold on long questions | Sticky at the bottom of the screen; content scrolled into view always clears it |
| Map | Zig-zag cards, no path; every closed level repeated "Cửa này còn khóa! Con xong Màn n là mở nha."; friends' faces overlapped the level name | A dotted trail through the levels (solid once walked); only the next closed level says why, the rest show a number and a lock (screen readers still hear the reason); a ▶ on the open level; friends' faces inside the card; the homework is a side stop on the trail after level 3; the four friends wait at the end |
| Map header | Two rows: name + save, then homework + "Không phải An?" | "Chào An!" with "Không phải An?" under it; the team jar is one compact card |
| Profile picker | Plain grid | The four characters greet the children; each face sits on its own soft colour |
| Intro | Speech line without a tail | A soft spotlight behind the character; the bubble points at it |
| Cards | Plain white card; large parent-hint box | The level's character peeks over the card (static, DR-05); the parent hint is one small line |
| Quiz | Other options stayed bright after the answer; the mascot in the feedback box shrank to about 25 px next to long text | Options neither picked nor right step back; the mascot keeps its size |
| Quiz end, done | | A spotlight behind the mascot; candies pop in one by one; sun rays behind the sticker |
| Worksheet | A missing part was marked only by a red line under it | The field itself gets an orange border too |
| Review | "✏️ Sửa" wrapped into a tall round button | A compact pill; question numbers in colour |
| Homework | A full-width "In" button above the task | A small "🖨️ In" beside the title (printing is for the parent) |

## Bug found and fixed

After a quiz answer, the explanation was meant to scroll into view on a phone. It never did:
the check ran only while the attempt was unsaved, and the attempt is saved before the first
render. It now scrolls once, right after the answer, and stops above the sticky Next button.

## Unchanged on purpose

- No new motion on card screens (DR-05); the progress bar does not animate.
- Every animation is still off under reduced motion.
- Tap targets stay at least 48 × 48 px, including "Không phải An?".
- Text colours are the existing tokens (contrast as in DR-04); the trail colour is decoration only.
- No new copy except the map greeting "Chào {name}!" (`hello`, can be overridden in `ui`).
